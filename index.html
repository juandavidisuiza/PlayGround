How to play

One lane. Ten mana. Four magical maidens. Topple the enemy Crown Towers, then the Queen Tower.

  • Mana refills over time (faster in overtime). Each card costs mana.
  • Deploy by tapping a card, then tapping your half of the arena. Or drag the card onto the lane.
  • Crown Towers shield the Queen. Each Crown Tower destroyed = 1 crown.
  • Win by destroying the Queen Tower, or by leading on crowns when time runs out. Overtime is sudden death.
